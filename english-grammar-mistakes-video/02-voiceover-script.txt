# VOICEOVER SCRIPT — "5 English Grammar Mistakes You Might Make Every Day"
# Clean narration, one sentence per line. Total runtime ~5:40.
# [Alex:] / [Maya:] = character dialogue. Parentheticals are timing/beat notes.

[1. OPENING HOOK — 0:00–0:15]
Do you say, "I am agree," "She don't like it," or "I didn't went"?
These mistakes are more common than you think.
In the next few minutes, you'll learn five simple corrections
that can make your English sound much more natural.
And number five is a mistake you might make every single day!

[2. SET UP THE CHALLENGE — 0:15–0:35]
Meet Alex.
Alex knows lots of English words, but he keeps making five small grammar mistakes.
His friend Maya thinks she can catch them all.
Can you spot the mistakes before Maya does?
Let's find out.

[3. MISTAKE ONE — 0:35–1:20]
Alex: I am agree with you!
Maya: Wait! Just say, "I agree with you."
Agree is already a verb. We don't need "am" before it.
Which is correct: "I agree" or "I am agree"?
(pause 2 seconds)
It's "I agree." No "am."

[4. MISTAKE TWO — 1:20–2:05]
Alex: She don't play football.
Maya: She doesn't play football!
With he, she, and it, we use "doesn't" in the present simple negative.
He doesn't run. It doesn't work.
Alex: She don't... wait — She doesn't play football!

[5. MISTAKE THREE — 2:05–2:50]
"I didn't went" is wrong.
After "didn't," use the base form of the verb.
Go, eat, see — not went, ate, saw.
Mini-quiz: "I didn't ___ breakfast." Eat or ate?
Three... two... one...
"I didn't eat breakfast." The base form is "eat."

[6. MISTAKE FOUR — 2:50–3:35]
"I have 25 years" is wrong.
We talk about age with "be," not "have."
"I am 25 years old." "She is 30."

[7. MISTAKE FIVE — 3:35–4:25]
"He go to school every day" is wrong.
In the present simple, add -s or -es to most verbs with he, she, or it.
She works. He watches TV. It rains.
Help Alex: "He ___ to school every day."
He goes to school every day!

[8. RAPID-FIRE CHALLENGE — 4:25–5:15]
Correct each sentence. Three... two... one...
"She don't understand." She doesn't understand.
"I didn't saw him." I didn't see him.
"He play football every day." He plays football every day.
How many did you get? Share your score!

How many did you get right — three, four, or all five?
