## 4. Method & caveats

- **Data:** latest ~15 shorts per channel via YouTube’s generated Shorts RSS
  (`UUSH` playlists); views/likes via public per-video stats (watch pages +
  ReturnYouTubeDislike). Jynxzi/Dream likes unavailable (shelf counts, rounded).
- **Momentum score (0–100):** per short, `0.55·velocity + 0.25·engagement +
  0.20·recency`; channel score = top-6 average + cadence bonus (up to +8),
  ×0.9 if dormant. Full formula in `shorts-tracker/shorts_tracker.py`.
- **Projections are naive** (median views/day × 14, linear): real shorts decay,
  and one viral outlier can drag medians. Treat projections as scale indicators.
- Shorts younger than ~3 hours have noisy views/day (Kreek’s 26K-in-1-hour short
  scores 100 on velocity but will settle as it ages).
- Snapshots age fast — re-run `shorts_tracker.py` (with `channels.json`) any day
  to refresh; the dashboard/report regenerate automatically.
