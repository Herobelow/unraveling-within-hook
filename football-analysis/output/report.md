# Football outcome probabilities: weekend of 17-18 October 2026

As of 2026-10-16 (UTC). Generated by `football-analysis/predict.py`.

**Scope:** Top-5 European leagues, weekend of 17-18 Oct 2026 (UTC), plus Opta-only Man City v Ipswich (Sat 17) and Coventry v Tottenham (Mon 19). Champions League matchday 3 (20-21 Oct) is covered in the news log only. Championship, League Two and African/Kenyan leagues are not covered.

**Method:** baseline 1X2 probabilities (MatchSharp market-implied where available, otherwise Opta) are shifted by news factors (`effect x confidence`, log-odds units). A Poisson goal model is then fitted to the adjusted 1X2 to give expected goals, Over 2.5 and BTTS. Probabilities are estimates, not guarantees, and this is not betting advice.

**Caveats:** (1) Market baselines already include widely known team news, so factor adjustments only add information the market may not yet price. Treat them as a judgement overlay. (2) Factor sizes are analyst judgement, not fitted to historical data. (3) Opta-only matches may already include rest effects, so some congestion adjustments there risk double counting. (4) Independent Poisson ignores the low-score correlation that Dixon-Coles corrects, so draw probabilities may be slightly understated.
